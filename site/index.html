4 things I learned working next to 500+ launches.

My business partner has launched over 500 Airbnbs. These are the lessons that stuck with me most.

01

The property is not the product. The stay is.

Guests don't remember your square footage. They remember how the place made them feel the second they walked in.

02

Your first 10 reviews decide your next 100 bookings.

Over deliver early. Check in more, fix things fast. Those first reviews carry the listing for years.

03

Photos are your storefront.

Cheap photos are the most expensive mistake in this business. Nobody books a home they can't picture themselves in.

04

Systems beat hustle.

Hustle gets you the first launch. Systems got him to 500. Same rule I live by in the gym.
