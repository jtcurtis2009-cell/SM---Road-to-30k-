## Instagram caption

500+ Airbnb launches taught me 5 things nobody tells new hosts.

When I launched my first short term rental, I thought the house was the product. Nice furniture, good location, done.

I was wrong.

Eleven years and over 500 launches later, the hosts who win are not the ones with the nicest homes. They're the ones who treat the stay like an experience, guard their first reviews, price every single day, invest in real photos, and build systems instead of grinding harder.

That last one goes way past real estate. Systems are how I train, how I run my week, and how I'm building this page.

This is day one of the Road to 30K. I'm sharing everything I've learned about STRs, mindset and discipline, out loud.

## LinkedIn version

After 11 years and 500+ Airbnb launches, here are 5 lessons I wish someone had handed me on day one:

1. The property is not the product. The stay is.
2. Your first 10 reviews decide your next 100 bookings.
3. Pricing is a daily job, not a setting.
4. Photos are your storefront. Cheap ones cost the most.
5. Systems beat hustle.

Number 5 is the one I come back to in everything, from launches to training to how I plan my week.

Which one would you add to the list?
